How it works

Split the $10,000 across 5 slices: Equal, Upsell, Reviews, Recovery, Teammate.

Give a bigger percentage to what you think matters most. The page does the math and shows who gets what.

Equal

Everyone gets the same, no matter what.

Example: 40% in Equal = $4,000 ÷ 6 agents = about $667 each, even for someone who sold nothing.
